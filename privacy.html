Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 2025

DailyCoach (“the App”) is developed and operated by Ayden DeNeen. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • User-entered data — your goals, reasons, obstacles, and coaching messages.
  • Progress and streak data — how often you complete actions or sessions.
  • Subscription status — information about whether you have an active subscription, as provided by Apple.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate coaching responses and daily action plans.
  • To track your streaks and progress over time.
  • To determine access to premium features (DailyCoach+).
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

3. Data Storage

Your personal data (goals, messages, streaks) is primarily stored on your device. Some data, such as subscription status, may be synced via Apple services. DailyCoach does not sell your data to third parties.

4. Payments & Subscriptions

DailyCoach+ subscriptions are managed and billed through Apple using in-app purchases. The App does not store or have access to your full payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings.

5. AI & Processing

The App may use AI-based logic to help generate coaching-style suggestions. Your text input may be processed for this purpose, but it is not sold or shared for advertising.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 16. If you are under 16, please do not use the App.

7. Your Rights

  • You can delete the App at any time, which removes locally stored data from your device.
  • You may contact the developer to request help or ask questions about your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When updated, the “Effective Date” at the top will be revised.
